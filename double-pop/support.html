FAQ

  • How do I play? Slide to aim and lift your finger to drop a piece into the jar. When two pieces with the same number touch, they pop and merge into one piece with double the value — all the way from 2 to 2048.
  • When does the game end? If pieces stay above the dashed line for a couple of seconds, the jar is full.
  • What is Second Chance? Once per game you can watch an optional short video; the top of the jar is cleared and you keep playing. You can always tap “No thanks”.
  • How do I change my ad consent? Use the Privacy button on the main menu (in regions where consent is required) or iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • My best score is gone. Scores are stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them too.
